# HKMC comparison v0.4.6

The existing 香港年金_領取金額及百分比 tab is read without modifying its schema. A–D are the 52 reference payouts; G–Q contain owner-supplied screenshot observations for 60/65 male/female. S–Y are not an input to the app. Observations are arithmetic-validated and labelled reference, not independently verified individual policy quotes. For other eligible issue ages, cash values are unavailable; no 60/65 curve is substituted. The income and values APIs fail safely when rows are missing, duplicated or inconsistent.

Calculations assume an in-force policy, no withdrawal, payments for 12 months per completed policy year, and a claim at that boundary. The premium is proportionally modelled from the HK$1m monthly example; amounts are rounded only for display, and premium-scaled cash values and benefit are rounded to whole HKD. Neither scaling nor rounding is confirmed as an individual official quotation. Within the guarantee period the greater of cash value and premium less paid income is used. After 105% of premium has been paid the standard death benefit is zero, subject to claim and policy terms. Monthly income continues thereafter. The AIA output uses its existing schedule.

The owner must review gas/Code.gs, preserve EVERBLISS_SHEET_ID and AVA_PLATFORM_ADMIN_ENDPOINT Script Properties, update the existing web-app deployment only after explicit approval, and verify both getHKAnnuityRates and getHKAnnuityValues against the current sheet. No production GAS was deployed or Sheet edited. The new reader is not available through an older live deployment; until updated the app shows unavailable values. Desktop, iPad, and actual devices remain unverified. The screenshots behind G–Q and the exact premium-scaling and payment-rounding rules remain to be independently verified with HKMC.
